# Millora Business Suite — Complete User Guide & Manual
*(A Simple, Non-Technical Guide for Shop Owners, Cashiers, and Print Center Managers)*

---

## 🌟 Welcome to Millora

**Millora** is your all-in-one digital business assistant. It was specifically built for local retail shops, copy centers, cybercafés, stationery stores (*librairies*), and convenience stores.

Instead of needing five different complicated software programs or messy paper notebooks, Millora gives you everything in one clean, easy-to-use screen:
1. **A Fast Cash Register (POS)** to ring up sales and print receipts.
2. **Stock & Inventory Control** to know exactly what you have in stock and how much profit you make.
3. **A Digital Debt Book (*Kounach / Carnet de Dettes*)** to track customer credit and payments without losing a single dinar.
4. **Sales & Profit Reports** to see your daily earnings, net profit, and best-selling items at a glance.
5. **An Educational Print Hub** with over 44,000 Algerian school exams ready to view and print with one click.
6. **A PDF Document Merger** to combine multiple exam sheets or customer files into one document.
7. **Mobile-to-PC File DropZone** so customers can send you documents directly from their smartphone without cables, USB keys, or WhatsApp.
8. **A QR Code Studio** to create custom QR codes for your Wi-Fi, social media, payment info, and business cards.

## 🛒 Module 1: POS Cashier (The Cash Register)

The **POS Cashier** is your primary day-to-day screen when customers are in your store.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
|  [Search product or scan barcode...]   [Categories Pills]   |
+------------------------------------+------------------------+
|                                    | YOUR SHOPPING CART     |
|   [Product 1]     [Product 2]      | ---------------------- |
|   150 DA          300 DA           | 2x Product 1 = 300 DA  |
|                                    | 1x Product 2 = 300 DA  |
|   [Product 3]     [Product 4]      | ---------------------- |
|   50 DA           1,200 DA         | Total: 600 DA          |
|                                    | [ Discount % ]         |
|   [Product 5]     [Product 6]      | ---------------------- |
|   80 DA           450 DA           | [💵 Espèces] [💳 CIB]  |
|                                    | [📲 Baridi]  [🏦 Dette]|
+------------------------------------+------------------------+
```

### 1. Adding Items to the Order
- **Using a Barcode Scanner Gun:** Simply scan any item's barcode. The item immediately beeps and appears in the cart on the right.
- **Using Camera Scanner:** If you don't have a handheld barcode gun, you can activate your laptop or PC webcam to scan barcodes directly.
- **Using the Search Bar:** Type the first few letters of a product's name (e.g., "cahier", "stylo", "jus") or its barcode.
- **Clicking the Product Grid:** Click directly on any product card or filter by category buttons (e.g., *Beverages, Supplies, Printing, Bakery*).

### 2. Managing the Cart
- **Change Quantity:** Click the **+** or **−** buttons next to an item in the cart.
- **Remove an Item:** Click the trash icon to remove a line.
- **Clear Everything:** Click "Clear Cart" if the customer changes their mind.
- **Apply a Discount:** Enter a discount percentage (e.g., `10%` or `5%`) to automatically recalculate the new price.

### 3. Choosing the Payment Method
When the customer is ready to pay, choose one of four payment options:
1. **💵 Cash (*Espèces*):** The standard cash checkout. Type the amount handed to you by the customer, and Millora instantly calculates the exact change to give back.
2. **💳 Bank Card (*CIB / Edahabia*):** For card payments via your POS electronic payment terminal.
3. **📲 Mobile BaridiMob / QR:** Displays a QR code on screen. The customer scans it with their BaridiMob app to copy your RIP or pay directly.
4. **🏦 Customer Credit (*Dette / À Crédit*):** If a trusted regular customer wants to take items and pay later, select their name from your customer list. The sale is finished, the stock is updated, and the balance is automatically added to their digital debt ledger.

### 4. Printing the Receipt (*Ticket de Caisse*)
As soon as the checkout is validated:
- Millora automatically deducts the sold items from your inventory.
- A clean, professional receipt is generated with your shop name, date/time, itemized list, totals, and barcode.
- It can print silently to your standard 80mm or 58mm thermal receipt printer.

## 📦 Module 2: Inventory & Stock Management

This module helps you keep track of everything you sell, what it costs you, and when you need to order more.

### 1. Adding a New Product
Click **"Add New Product"** and fill in:
- **Product Name:** (e.g., *Ramette Papier A4 80g*).
- **Category:** (e.g., *Bureautique, Boissons, Fournitures*).
- **Cost Price (*Prix d'achat*):** What you paid the supplier for one unit (e.g., `650 DA`).
- **Selling Price (*Prix de vente*):** What you charge customers (e.g., `850 DA`).
- **Live Profit Preview:** The system immediately shows your exact profit per unit (e.g., `+200 DA (23.5% margin)`).
- **Current Stock:** How many units you have on your shelves right now (e.g., `50`).
- **Low Stock Alert Threshold:** The minimum number before a warning appears (e.g., alert me when stock reaches `5`).
- **Barcode:** Scan the product's barcode or type it in.

### 2. Low Stock Warnings
Whenever a product drops below your minimum threshold, it is highlighted in **amber/red** so you can reorder from your suppliers before running out completely.

### 3. Stock Movement History (Audit Trail)
Every time an item is sold or restocked, Millora writes an automatic, tamper-proof log:
- Who changed it, the date and time, whether it was a sale or a restock, the previous stock, and the new stock.

## 🏦 Module 3: Customer Debt Ledger (*Kounach / Carnet de Dettes*)

In many Algerian shops and neighborhoods, trusted customers buy on credit and settle their balance at the end of the week or month. Paper notebooks get lost, damaged, or suffer from calculation errors. Millora completely solves this.

### 1. Customer Profiles
- Create a profile for each customer with their **Full Name**, **Phone Number**, and an optional **Credit Limit** (maximum debt allowed).
- See their total unpaid balance at a single glance.

### 2. Selling on Credit
- At the POS cashier, select the customer's name and choose **"Dette"**.
- The purchase is added to their account with the exact date, time, and list of items purchased.

### 3. Recording Payments (*Règlement de Dette*)
When the customer comes to pay:
- Open their profile.
- Enter the amount they are paying (Full payment or partial payment, e.g. `2,000 DA` out of `5,000 DA`).
- Millora instantly updates the remaining balance and generates a printable payment receipt to give to the customer.

### 4. Overdue & Dormant Account Warnings
Accounts with old unpaid balances are clearly flagged so you remember to follow up.

## 📲 Module 7: Mobile-to-PC File Reception DropZone

One of the biggest daily headaches in a print shop or cybercafé is receiving files from customers:
- Customers using USB flash drives that might contain computer viruses.
- Having to add customers to WhatsApp or Bluetooth.
- Slow email forwarding.

Millora provides a modern **QR-based File DropZone**:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
|               HOW CUSTOMER FILE TRANSFER WORKS              |
|                                                             |
|  1. Customer scans QR code on your counter screen with phone |
|  2. Their phone browser opens the secure shop upload page   |
|  3. They select their PDF, Word docs, or photos (up to 250MB)|
|  4. Files appear instantly on your PC screen ready to print |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Cashier Workflow:
1. Click **"Mobile File Drop"** on your screen.
2. A large QR code appears on your screen (or on a printed stand on your counter).
3. The customer points their phone camera at the QR code and taps the link.
4. The customer picks their documents and taps "Upload".
5. Within 2 seconds, the files appear in your list with full previews and a **"Print Now"** button.
6. When done, you can delete the files with 1 click to keep your storage clean.

## ⚙️ Settings, Backups & Data Safety

Your business records are valuable. Millora ensures you never lose your data.

### 1. One-Click Backup (Recommended Every Week)
- Go to **Settings & Data Safety**.
- Click **"Export Database Backup (.json)"**.
- A small backup file is saved to your computer or USB drive containing all your products, sales history, customer debt records, and stock logs.

### 2. Restoring Data (Moving to a New PC)
- If you buy a new computer, install Millora on the new PC.
- Go to **Settings** → **"Restore Database"** → Select your backup file.
- Everything is restored in 2 seconds.

### 3. Automatic 7-Day Backup Reminder
If 7 days pass without exporting a backup, Millora displays a gentle reminder banner at the top of your screen to keep your data safe.

### 4. Excel / Spreadsheet Export
Need to send your numbers to an accountant or view them in Microsoft Excel?
- Click **"Export to Excel / CSV"** for your **Product Catalog**, **Sales History**, or **Customer Debt Book**.

## 📋 A Typical Day with Millora (Step-by-Step Guide)

### 🌅 Morning: Opening the Shop
1. Turn on your computer and open Millora (it launches automatically or by clicking the desktop icon).
2. Check the **Sales & Analytics** tab to review any items in the **Low Stock Warning** list.
3. If new merchandise arrived, go to **Inventory & Stock** and update your quantities.

### ☀️ Midday: Serving Customers
- **Customer buying school supplies:** Scan items with the barcode gun $\rightarrow$ Click **"Espèces"** $\rightarrow$ Hand the customer their receipt and change.
- **Parent asking for 4th Year Middle School Math Exams:** Click **Print Hub** $\rightarrow$ Type `"Math 4AM"` $\rightarrow$ Click **Print** $\rightarrow$ Collect payment.
- **Customer wanting to print a CV from their phone:** Point them to the **Mobile Drop QR code** on your counter $\rightarrow$ File appears on your PC $\rightarrow$ Click **Print**.
- **Neighborhood regular asking to pay at the end of the month:** Ring up their items $\rightarrow$ Choose **"Dette"** $\rightarrow$ Select their name $\rightarrow$ Done.

### 🌙 Evening: Closing the Register
1. Go to **Sales & Analytics** $\rightarrow$ Select **"Today"**.
2. Compare the **Gross Revenue** with the physical cash in your drawer.
3. Take note of your **Net Profit** earned today.
4. Once a week, click **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **"Export Backup"** and save the file onto a USB thumb drive.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q1: Does Millora require an internet connection?
**No.** All essential features—cash register, stock, debt management, analytics, exam printing, and QR generation—work 100% offline.

#### Q2: Can I use a regular barcode scanner gun?
**Yes.** Any USB or wireless barcode scanner gun works out of the box. Just plug it in and start scanning.

#### Q3: Can I connect multiple computers or a tablet on the same Wi-Fi?
**Yes.** While the main computer runs Millora, any tablet, laptop, or phone connected to the same local Wi-Fi network can open the app using the computer's local address (e.g., `http://192.168.1.15:3000`).

#### Q4: What happens if my computer crashes or I get a new one?
As long as you saved your weekly backup file (from the Settings tab), you can install Millora on any other computer, click "Restore", and resume business in under one minute with all your products, sales, and debt books intact.

#### Q5: Is my financial and customer data private?
**100% Private.** Unlike cloud software that uploads your data to unknown servers, Millora stores everything strictly on your local computer's drive.
